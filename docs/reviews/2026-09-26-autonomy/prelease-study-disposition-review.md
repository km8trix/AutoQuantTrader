## Why stopping this branch is justified

The approved proposal explicitly permits rejection when the limited useful work does not justify the required complete design and preserved fresh checks. The source study identified CLOCK canonicality as the smallest finite candidate and reserved this observation to determine whether further qualification was warranted. That question now has concrete evidence: the entire selected original codec region occupies fewer than nine milliseconds in the completed workload. Additional preparation, fresh exact matching, qualified behavior checking, acquisition ownership and cleanup would have to be accounted for; none may be treated as free. No exact cost estimate for those additions is needed to decide not to pursue this particular engineering design for such a small observed opportunity.

Do not call 8.204370 ms an established saving: a prepared first decode, cold reflection/cache behavior, all matching/qualification and transaction cleanup costs were not measured. Do not claim the proposed implementation would fail its resource bounds or necessarily run slower. Neither claim was tested. The justified disposition is that its measured scope is immaterial to the present restore problem and does not warrant the remaining architectural work.

## Unresolved matters and durable next boundary

The study has not established a production acquisition witness for the transaction's complete locked predecessor and committed result, cleanup ownership after successful commit but before return, a safe old/new snapshot comparison permitting only the operation's precise two-row acquisition delta, or qualified consumption by the newly constructed original owners. CLOCK reuse also still lacks a complete warm/cold typing-cache, schema, constructor, native codec and callback predicate; complete overlapping resource accounting; adverse handoff acceptance; and preparation-through-cleanup benefit. Those remain explicitly unresolved rather than disproven.

After rejecting this candidate, no further witness model, behavior framework, reuse prototype, repeat fixture or cap increase is necessary to complete this bounded study branch. Preserve the source studies, rejected-candidate rationale, frozen observer/selfchecks, successful original result and its limits. Keep the existing production implementation and original fresh SQL/object/source/fence/final/cleanup checks unchanged. This local diagnostic pass does not repair or accept the known exact-source Linux failures or the earlier attempt-proof performance result. A materially different subset would require a new concrete assessment of its own scope and evidence; it is not automatically authorized production work and should not be invented merely to avoid recording a valid rejection.
