# Proposed separate daily identity feasibility experiment

**Approved for feasibility only, 2026-09-26.** The owner explicitly answered
“Approve feasibility experiment only” to this proposal. The approved
attempt-fingerprint pilot preserves both daily snapshot validations. This
separate experiment concerns that different daily predicate. It authorizes no production substitution,
provider access, account initialization, deployment, risk increase or live effect.

## Evidence and recommendation

The [original-only measurement](daily-identity-original-result-assessment.md)
completed all 24 borrows and 144 selected checks. Identity construction cost
2.1675 s of a 44.306 s local instrumented execute; whole public checks cost
2.4653 s inclusively. New proof costs would reduce any possible saving. The
unchanged candidate still fails Linux's original 60-second lease gates.

The validated vector has 54,690 edges. Simultaneous attempt-proof usage leaves
64,813 binding and 8,997 container units within the existing caps. A design with
one binding per edge has only 10,123 further binding units before additional
behavior, context and temporary charges. The 9,356 original visited objects and
9,923 unknown histogram edges are not an admissibility or resource inventory.
Neither sufficient benefit nor shared-budget fit is established.

Do not wire a new daily proof into production on this evidence. A feasibility-only
experiment is the smallest concrete next scope if the owner wants to investigate
this boundary further; rejection is a successful outcome for that experiment.

## Exact proposed scope

- Use one source-owned synthetic snapshot and a temporary, bounded data/behavior
  prototype of the **daily all-field identity predicate**, compared against the
  literal original helper. Do not reuse the attempt fingerprint's field selector.
- Examine only the two existing daily check positions inside authentic original
  factory borrows as a possible future seam. Do not alter public/prepared methods,
  registration, transaction callers, full entry/final validation or any existing
  SQL/object/fence/read/cleanup schedule.
- Keep original owner/seal identity checks. Qualified post-issuance schema or
  behavior mutation would reject; unsupported initial shape uses the original
  validator. This stricter qualified-profile contract is the consequential change
  being investigated, not a claim of universal helper equivalence.
- Account jointly for the existing attempt proof and all proposed data, behavior,
  owner/context and construction state under 16,384 containers/131,072 bindings.
  No cap increase, cross-proof deduplication framework, lease renewal, replay or
  larger timeout is allowed.
- Compare complete prototype construction and all fresh-use/retirement costs with
  the original identity oracle on the same finite data. Include independent
  mutation, alias/cycle, class/schema, copied-owner and resource-bound oracles.
  Preserve callback/error compatibility for unsupported inputs.
- Stop if the original predicate cannot be proved by a finite exact profile,
  joint resources exceed the original caps, or complete savings are absent or
  too uncertain to justify a larger genuine lifecycle experiment. Report an
  honest rejection without modifying production or repeatedly retuning the run.

## Deliverable and further boundary

Deliver source-bound temporary code, adverse mechanics results, complete cost and
joint-resource accounting, and an independent accept/reject feasibility review.
This experiment cannot close A2, establish Linux margin or authorize factory
wiring. Any proposed production adoption must return as a concrete separately
reviewable change with original acceptance gates, under the
[full boundary assessment](daily-identity-proof-decision-assessment.md).

## Completed first gate

The owner-approved feasibility work rejected the concrete
[per-node layout](daily-identity-node-layout.md) using the existing authentic
measurement. Its joint lower bound is 16,743 containers/139,661 bindings: over
the original caps by 359/8,589 before all nonnegative omitted costs. The
[scalar screen](daily-identity-node-layout-resource-screen.json) and
[independent review](daily-identity-node-screen-independent-review.md) preserve
the source/hash/accounting evidence; 37 mechanics cases pass.

Under this proposal's early-stop rule, no graph admission, complete behavior
proof, timing prototype or new genuine fixture was needed or implemented.
Complete-cost deliverables apply only to a layout that first fits. This rejects
the stated representation, not all possible proofs; no alternative packing or
sharing framework and no production substitution follows automatically.
