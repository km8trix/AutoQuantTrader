## Exact supported seam and remaining qualification

`durable_journal.py:_validate_record` first calls `record.__post_init__`, looks up the allowlisted root, then invokes the codec decode, exact-type check and re-encode comparison. `personal_codec.decode_record` itself performs an internal re-encode/canonical-byte comparison. A CLOCK verdict would justify only the admitted successful typed predicate; it cannot erase the outer record/allowlist checks, claim a skipped callback ran, or remove either original encode without establishing the same predicate under a qualified profile. Measuring the whole `_validate_record` call gives an upper bound for the narrower removable work, not its exact cost.

The nominal CLOCK object graph is finite: `RuntimeClockObservation`, `ContinuousAccountScope` and `VersionPin`, plus their scalar/tuple/optional fields and inherited contract validation. Finite nominal data does not automatically give finite behavior coverage. Generated constructors, `__post_init__`, inherited annotations, field and slot descriptors, policy contents/equality, datetime parsing/UTC behavior, typing/dataclass helpers, both canonical encodes and native JSON paths must be enumerated from actual source. A broad generic behavior-attestation framework is neither necessary nor justified by this checkpoint; unknown shapes can remain ordinary fallback.

There is a concrete cache issue, not merely a hypothetical monkeypatch objection: codec `_hints` is an LRU wrapper returning a mutable type-hint dictionary; contracts also maintain mutable `_TYPE_HINTS`. Wrapper identity plus a once-observed dictionary does not prove a later original call sees that dictionary. Eviction or `cache_clear()` can restore cold reflection. A bounded profile covering both the relevant warm dictionary contents and original cold reflection could make residency irrelevant. Its sufficiency remains to be proved; cache identity alone is insufficient. This is ordinary qualification work within the approved study, not evidence that the three-record candidate is impossible.

Behavior/error obligations include the original constructor rejection and canonical-byte rules, exact schema/root qualification, nested mutation and descriptor/type changes, cache mutation/clear/eviction, original StopIteration/RecursionError/exception translation boundaries, and temporary-object lifetimes. Resource-exhaustion equivalence should not be invented beyond established contracts, but the optimization cannot bypass supported ordinary validation or conceal failures. The custom codec public API remains unchanged; unsupported behavior follows the original path.

## Acquisition and cleanup are a separate gate

The acquisition study identifies a real predecessor gap: the existing conditional acquisition locks/checks the head, inserts generation G+1 revision 1, updates/readbacks the head, and commits before returning, but returns no original full-predecessor/committed-result witness. Equal externally supplied or later-read facts are not such a witness. Its proposed same-owner/thread/operation scope must bind the actual locked predecessor, exact preparation and exact committed lease/result. Existing public acquisition and freshness checks must remain intact.

The post-COMMIT/pre-delivery interval must have explicit cleanup ownership. Preallocating an inert scope and retaining an original cleanup holder before COMMIT is a plausible finite protocol, not a verified one. Rollback cannot expose authority; confirmed acquisition cannot be stranded by witness activation/delivery failure; uncertain commit outcome cannot be resolved by invented matching-row authority. This must be designed and tested before actual reuse consumes a witness. It does not prevent a scalar phase/ABA/cleanup counterexample model, which must label all identities as inert model labels and prove no SQL or production ownership.

Legal acquire/release ABA changes generation and immutable history; comparison may allow only the witnessed new lease row and exact target head replacement while requiring the rest of the fresh dependency inventory to match. No entire-table exemption, fence renewal, snapshot rebasing or receipt reconstruction follows. A clock-only verdict still needs this coherent handoff if it originates before acquisition.

## Cost and resource rejection gates

The existing profile's all-schema journal/codec totals are overlapping and partial; they establish neither selected CLOCK cost nor repeats. Record both successful and unwound original calls and separate execution extent. An original-only measured pass establishes observed workload cost, not candidate speedup, future Linux headroom or completed prototype qualification. A failed/partial observation cannot be extrapolated to a completed restore.

The candidate's total-work equation must charge selected pre-decode, added coherent capture/comparison, behavior qualification, per-use matching, witness delivery, failed attempts and retirement. Moving once-only work before a lease may reduce lease-held duration while increasing total work; repeated successful original computations are the concrete opportunity to measure. Fixed whole-call timings are only a ceiling unless the narrower codec children are also observed without double counting.

Every simultaneous original, preparation, fresh, behavior, witness and temporary structure must fit the existing structural, binding, SQL-transfer, byte, wall/CPU/memory and lease limits. No separate preparation pool, timer relocation, renewal, digest-as-authority shortcut or free alias/copy assumption is allowed. A failure of a specific complete accounting screen should reject that layout. No such measured screen is asserted here.
