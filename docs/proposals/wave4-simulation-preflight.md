## Narrowest reuse

- Keep `PersonalAccounting.advance/project` and its existing `AccountingState`, immutable commands, order/ledger/FIFO/settlement/action reducers as the only economic implementation. Its observation path already supports deterministic whole-share partial quantities, remaining capacity and strictly later activation/frontier checks (`packages/backtest/personal_accounting.py:901`). Preserve exact Decimal money and model-fee arithmetic.
- Add a persistent simulated-venue facade beside the old simulator. It records accepted submission/cancel/control/observation commands and resulting authoritative venue facts before answering. Its separate account state is reconstructed from **its own journal**, using the same accounting reducers; never copy the coordinator's ending balance/positions to manufacture agreement. A consumes paginated venue facts through its reconciliation interface; B owns authorization, capacity and dispatch fencing. Common reducer reuse is not an independent economic oracle: retain hand-calculated journal/cash cases as well as cross-store reconciliation checks.
- Do not promote `ConservativeSimulatedBroker` to the continuous venue: it preloads a finite tape and returns acceptance plus the first later full fill in `submit` (`simulated_broker.py:1293,1404`). Preserve this legacy contract and its callers.
- Keep `run_causal_engine` as the historical compatibility wrapper. The current implementation creates fresh strategy/trigger/feature/queue state and product admission requires an empty initial account (`causal_engine.py:120,406,1372`); passing yesterday's `AccountingState` into repeated historical runs is not restart support. Root must extract a resumable frontier operation **within the same causal engine**, retaining stage 5→6 serialization and stage 7 activation. C must not copy `_Engine`, calculate strategies, or implement another scheduler.
- Reuse W1 Tiingo response validation, request/response transport records, exact-byte hashes and receipt conventions. The small personal capture already separates request, receipt and validated availability (`scripts/capture_personal_tiingo.py:73,125`). Its importer/input builder remains current-vintage/modelled (`tiingo_eod_import.py:193`; `personal_inputs.py:68,190`), so it cannot be used unchanged to claim recorded forward availability. Existing `manifest_replay.py:99` deliberately excludes strategy callbacks; use its immutable identity/coverage patterns, not its market-only replay result as decision parity evidence.

## Root-owned interfaces required before dispatch

1. **Forward engine seam**, proposed `personal-forward-engine/1`: immutable `ForwardSpec`, `EngineCheckpoint`, `ClosedFrontier`, and `advance_frontier(checkpoint, frontier, *, accounting, strategy) -> FrontierTransition` in the canonical engine. Checkpoint binds account/deployment/model/build/calendar identity, accounting and strategy state, feature/revision heads, consumed trigger/target IDs, scheduled due events, last frontier/reduction sequence, and flow-neutral loss/valuation state. Transition carries accepted decisions/risk outcomes/commands and trace hashes. Restore validates the whole checkpoint against its retained input prefix; no initial-capital reapplication, forced terminal row or repeated callback at each poll. Root/B own durable checkpoint plus command consumption and account-fence transactions.
2. **Venue seam**, proposed `personal-stateful-venue/1`: `VenueCommand(command_id, account_id, environment, policy_sha256, dispatch_binding, payload)` and `VenueFact(venue_id, account_id, stream_id, sequence, predecessor_sha256, command_id, economic_at, observed_at, payload, payload_sha256)`. Payloads reuse canonical submissions/cancel requests/broker events/cash/action/settlement records. `apply(command, expected_head)` atomically returns its immutable receipt/facts or an exact retry; same ID/different payload rejects. `read_page(snapshot_id, cursor, limit)` binds all pages to one source head with explicit terminal coverage. Venue state persists independently of process memory. Mutation fences, simulated versus E*TRADE identity, read coverage, and object metadata are shared with A/B and frozen by root.
3. **Observed execution semantics**, separately versioned from `next-regular-open-proxy-v1` and `synthetic-events-v1`. Existing `ExecutionPolicy.model_id` and observation `basis` cannot truthfully represent bid/ask (`accounting_contracts.py:52,234`). Root owns the additive model/DTO and shared cost helper. Use eligible ask for buys and bid for sells plus explicitly pinned adverse model costs; missing/delayed/unqualified semantics reject. Quantity budgets, acceptance/cancel/receipt latency, late-fill ordering and expiry are deterministic **simulation assumptions**, never quote-size liquidity claims. W2 `_observe` currently excludes `pending_cancel`; root must provide explicit accepted-before-cancel execution fact ordering so delayed fills can still apply without pretending a cancel request confirmed cancellation. No price arithmetic clone in C.
4. **Forward capture seam**, proposed `personal-forward-capture/1`: `CaptureReceipt` binds provider/environment/local account scope where applicable, operation/instrument/session, request/receipt/validated UTC times, boot/monotonic interval, optional source timestamp/sequence and its documented scope, raw object hash/size, parser version, rights/retention/entitlement evidence references, and status/reasons. `ObservedQuote` has exact bid/ask and documented delay/session semantics; unknown fields stay null. `close_frontier(capture_head, expected_members, cutoff)` yields complete/skipped plus missing/revision reasons. Persist receipts, revisions and cursor/watermark atomically; crash retry cannot advance a cursor without retained bytes. Local capture sequence proves local ordering only. Late data appends knowledge and cannot reopen a consumed/skipped decision.

Root owns the shared DTO modules, canonical engine/accounting changes, financial/schema/migration ownership, codecs, application composition/CLI, build pins and API. A owns broker normalization/reconciliation; B owns account coordinator/risk/ownership. Reuse the content-addressed store's bounded no-clobber/fsync mechanism via a root-approved common object port; private store paths never enter semantic records.

## Actual-source gate and remaining gaps

The current `EtradeReadOperation` supports accounts/balances/portfolio/orders/activity only (`etrade_readonly.py:54,376`): **there is no implemented E*TRADE quote capture API**. Its signer/transport accept that exact account request type. Root/A must approve a narrowly typed quote GET seam that reuses session/environment isolation, deadline/response bounds and OAuth handling without opening arbitrary URLs or order endpoints. Do not add a second OAuth lifecycle or generic network client in C.

Before any actual capture, root obtains explicit scope authorization and reconfirms official endpoint/field semantics, selected production data entitlement/delay/source identity, permitted retention/replay, and provider quotas. W1's local ten-read/minute bound and successful account traversal prove neither a quote quota nor entitlement. Existing owner-declared daily-history research rights do not establish intraday quote rights. Tiingo EOD supplies no 09:35 quote. E*TRADE sandbox stored examples cannot satisfy this actual-session gate.

Capture one pinned regular-session cycle: preceding completed-bar decision attempt at 20:00 ET, factual arrivals/retries through the exclusive 09:00 next-session cutoff, actual authorized observations during 09:35 inclusive–09:40 exclusive, and session closing/coverage evidence. Freeze instruments, dates/calendar, strategy/policy and acquisition budgets first; retain private raw bytes and sanitized hashes/coverage. Existing source quote age `<5s`, receipt-to-check `<1s`, nonfuture source time and entitlement/account match must be evaluated from captured evidence. UTC health/monotonic uncertainty are root/B producers; unknown health blocks exposure while observation/recovery continue. A one-time historical download cannot stand in for this capture.

Replay retained observations **and control/clock-health/coverage facts** at their original knowledge frontiers. Compare canonical target/risk/intent decisions and economic transitions, excluding process attempt IDs, wall duration and generated-artifact timestamps. Compare uninterrupted execution with crashes before/after durable command, receipt and watermark commits. No-op replay with blocked quotes is useful negative evidence but does not alone demonstrate successful qualified venue execution; if entitlement or an actual session is unavailable, report that portion of W4 exit as blocked rather than substituting synthetic/next-open observations.

Minimum integrated cases: partial then cancel acknowledgement; execution economically before cancel but received later; duplicate/conflicting/corrected executions; explicit DAY expiry with uncertainty retained; manual simulated trade/cash/action; missing/reordered pages; skipped cutoff and late arrival; corrupted/missing capture object; restart at every journal boundary; two coordinator contenders; and stale mandatory inputs denying new exposure while cancellation/read/recovery continue. Require exact cash/settlement/fees/FIFO/positions/commitments against independent fixture calculations and A's reconciler. Production Place remains disabled; separately authorized E*TRADE read reconciliation is A's distinct gate.
